# Delete your Pocket Cat account and data

**App:** Pocket Cat for Android (`com.pocketcat.galchi`)

**Developer:** GitHub account `jhyang12345`

You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled Pocket Cat or no longer have your old device.

## Request deletion by email

[Email a Pocket Cat deletion request](mailto:jhyang123494@gmail.com?subject=Pocket%20Cat%20account%20and%20data%20deletion%20request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20Pocket%20Cat%20account%20and%20associated%20data.%0A%0AGoogle%20sign-in%20email%3A%20%0APurchase%20date%20or%20Google%20Play%20order%20ID%20%28optional%29%3A%20)

Or email **[jhyang123494@gmail.com](mailto:jhyang123494@gmail.com)** with the subject **Pocket Cat account and data deletion request**.

1. Tell us which Google email address you used to sign in to Pocket Cat. Send the request from that address if possible so we can verify ownership.
2. State whether you want your Pocket Cat account and associated data deleted, or only specified data.
3. If you bought gems, include an approximate purchase date or Google Play order ID if available. This helps locate RevenueCat records, including records from older anonymous installations. You can request deletion without making a purchase or having an order ID.

We may need to verify ownership before deleting an account. We will reply with any verification needed and confirmation of the result, including any records that cannot be removed and the reason. **Never send your Google password, payment-card details or purchase tokens.**

## What the request covers

- The Pocket Cat Firebase Authentication account, including its email and Google sign-in profile information.
- The current cloud save: your cat, Bond, gems, purchase-credit ledger, treats, cosmetics, saved gameplay preferences, postcard metadata and save-coordination metadata.
- RevenueCat customer and purchase records associated with the account or installation that we can identify and request to delete.

Firebase documents an Authentication deletion period of up to 180 days for live and backup systems. Google Play keeps payment records under Google's policies, and Android backups are controlled through your Google/Android backup settings. Deleting Pocket Cat data does not delete your Google account or provide a purchase refund.

We cannot remotely remove guest saves or Photo Mode pictures that exist only on a device. Clear Pocket Cat's app data or uninstall it to remove app-private local data. Delete exported photos separately from **Pictures/Pocket Cat** or your device's gallery.

## Delete the cloud account in the app

In Pocket Cat, open **Settings → Account & cloud save → Delete cloud account**, then confirm the same Google account you linked to your cat. Internet access is required. This removes the cloud save and Firebase account; your cat remains on the device as a guest.

For RevenueCat purchase-record deletion, or if the app reports that deletion could not finish, use the email request above. Signing out, uninstalling or clearing app data alone does not delete the cloud account.
